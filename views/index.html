Our Story

At Palmyra, we are passionate about creating delicious and healthy food options for our community. Our journey began amidst the challenges of the Syrian war, driven by a simple idea: to share the authentic flavors of our cherished family recipes with busy families and individuals who want to eat well but don't have the time or energy to cook. Starting from scratch, we have crafted a menu that honors our heritage and uses fresh, locally-sourced ingredients. With the help of our talented team, we have transformed this idea into a successful business that serves hundreds of happy customers every day.

Our Philosophy

We believe that food should be both delicious and nutritious. That's why we use only the freshest ingredients and prepare everything from scratch. Our falafel dough is made fresh from scratch, never imported or frozen, and cooked right away with freshly ground spices. Our hummus starts from fresh chickpeas, not canned, ensuring an authentic and vibrant taste. We pride ourselves on serving fresh food, not fast food. We are dedicated to conveying the rich culinary heritage of Syria, making it accessible to people anytime. Just like in Syria, where people can stop by and enjoy a light but fresh meal, we offer a variety of wholesome and flavorful options for those looking for a quick and healthy lunch, a family dinner, or catering for your next event. Whether you're craving a taste of home or exploring new flavors, we have you covered.
