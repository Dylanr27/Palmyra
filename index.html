A Bit About Us

At Palmyra, we are passionate about creating delicious and healthy food options for our community. Our journey began amidst the challenges of the Syrian war, driven by a simple idea: to share the authentic flavors of our cherished family recipes with busy families and individuals who want to eat well but don't have the time or energy to cook. Starting from scratch, we have crafted a menu that honors our heritage and uses fresh, locally-sourced ingredients. With the help of our talented team, we have transformed this idea into a successful business that serves hundreds of happy customers every day. Come visit us and experience the flavors of the Mediterranean for yourself!
